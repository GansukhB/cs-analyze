# Icons

`equipment/` and `deathnotice/` are Counter-Strike 2's own icons, extracted from the
game files (`panorama/images/icons/equipment/`, `panorama/images/hud/deathnotice/`)
by [Juknum/counter-strike-icons](https://github.com/Juknum/counter-strike-icons) and
minified with svgo (viewBox kept).

They are the property of Valve Corporation. Counter-Strike is a registered trademark
of Valve Corporation. They are used here for non-commercial, community and analysis
purposes only, as are the radar images in `../radars/`.
