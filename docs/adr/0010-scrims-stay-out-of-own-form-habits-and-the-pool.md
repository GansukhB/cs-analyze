# Scrims stay out of every Team's Own form, habits and the Pool

A Scrim is uploaded by one Workspace and recorded with a scrim partner. Its Maps get a Review and a Replay and count toward my team's own development (its Focus points, its Players' trends, how its Strats went), but never toward any Team's Own form, Playbook habits, Set plays, Self-scout, Rating or the Pool, and the scrim partner's play in it is never read into a Game Plan against them. Teams hide and test things in scrims, so their numbers would mislead a Game Plan, and preparing against a partner from their scrims would break the trust scrims run on. We chose this over counting Scrims like Matches, and over an opt-in toggle per Game Plan, knowing it leaves out data that is often plentiful for tier-2 Teams with few official Matches.

## Consequences

- Every query that builds Own form, habits, Set plays, Ratings or the Pool has to leave Scrims out, and so does every cache key computed from the Match Maps those read.
- Self-scout leaves Scrims out too: it shows what an opponent could prepare against, and opponents never see a Scrim.
