# Player rating stands beside Swing, not instead of it

The v2 pages show a Player rating, the published approximation of HLTV's Rating 2.0 (KAST, kills, deaths and assists per Round, Impact and ADR, with 1.00 an average game), wherever the design asks for a Player's number, including a Team's average of its five. We chose it over showing only Swing per Round because coaches and players read and compare that scale at a glance, and over leaving the number out, knowing that it is neither HLTV's own figure nor what the Review measures. Swing stays the number every judgement is made from: Findings, Focus points, Player cards, Strengths and Weaknesses, Levers and Cautions never read the Player rating, and the Player rating never says anything about a Team's strength, which is the Rating's.

## Consequences

- A page showing both calls the first "Rating" and the second "Swing"; the Team's Elo is called "Багийн хүч" on v2 so that "Rating" there always means a Player's.
- The formula is a fixed approximation: if HLTV publishes another, the old numbers are not comparable with the new ones.
